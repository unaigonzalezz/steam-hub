This key shows the game at this position in your library. Number your keys 1, 2, 3… and the profile fills itself in. A position past the end of the list stays blank.

Leave it empty and the key becomes the way in: it keeps the action icon and, when pressed, jumps to the bundled Steam profile.

Not every Elgato device ships with a ready-made profile yet. If yours is missing one, every action above still works, it just needs its keys laid out by hand, or a profile contributed by someone who owns that device, see the contributing guide.
